Understanding These Metrics

Completion and on-time rates depend on the selected task records and their recorded deadlines. Workload counts indicate task volume; they do not independently measure the quality or complexity of an employee's work.

This prototype calculates reports from locally stored demo data. Results are illustrative and should not be treated as verified company performance statistics.
